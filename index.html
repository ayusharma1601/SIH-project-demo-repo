Smart India Hackathon • Idea Overview

CarbonSaathi Pitch Summary

Mission LiFE-aligned Personal Carbon & Fitness Tracker

1. Problem Statement

Transport-related emissions in India are rising rapidly, but individuals get no real-time, actionable feedback on their daily commute. Existing calculators are manual and static.

2. Core Differentiation for India

  • Indian Transport Modes: Auto-rickshaw, e-rickshaw, shared auto, Indian metro & bus emission factors.
  • Hyperlocal Circles: College, Corporate office, and Housing society leaderboards to maximize organic engagement.
  • DPDP Act 2023 Privacy Layer: Raw GPS stays on device; only aggregated pseudonymous transit mode & distance sent.

3. Business & Monetization Model

B2B Corporate Wellness / ESG licenses (₹20-50/employee/month), Insurance partnerships for active lifestyle discounts, and CSR-funded green challenges.
